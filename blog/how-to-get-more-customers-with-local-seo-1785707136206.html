How to Get More Customers with Local SEO: A Practical Guide for Small Businesses

If you own a local business, you've probably wondered why some competitors show up at the top of Google while your website sits buried on page three. The answer, in most cases, comes down to local SEO — search engine optimization strategies specifically designed to help businesses rank higher in location-based searches. Whether you run a plumbing company, a dental practice, a boutique shop, or a restaurant, local SEO can be the difference between a slow week and a fully booked schedule. In this guide, we'll walk you through exactly how to get more customers using proven local SEO techniques that work.

What Is Local SEO and Why Does It Matter?

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your online presence so that your business appears prominently when people search for products or services near them. Think about the last time you typed something like "pizza near me" or "best plumber in Richmond, VA" into Google. The businesses that showed up at the top didn't land there by accident — they invested in local SEO.

According to Google, 46% of all searches have local intent, and 78% of local mobile searches result in an offline purchase within 24 hours. That means your potential customers are already searching for what you offer — the question is whether they're finding you or your competitors. Local SEO levels the playing field, allowing small businesses to compete with larger brands by targeting the right audience in the right geographic area.

Claim and Optimize Your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) — formerly known as Google My Business — is the single most important tool in your local SEO arsenal. This free listing is what powers the map results and the business information panel that appears when someone searches for your business or a service you provide.

Here's how to make the most of it:

  • Claim and verify your listing if you haven't already at business.google.com.
  • Fill out every field completely — business name, address, phone number, website, hours, and category.
  • Add high-quality photos of your storefront, products, team, and work. Listings with photos receive 42% more requests for directions.
  • Write a keyword-rich business description that clearly explains what you do and who you serve.
  • Post updates regularly — share promotions, news, and events to keep your profile active and engaging.
  • Enable messaging so customers can contact you directly from Google.

A fully optimized Google Business Profile dramatically increases your chances of appearing in the coveted "Local Pack" — the top three map results that get the lion's share of clicks on local searches.

Get More Reviews (and Respond to Every One)

Online reviews are one of the most powerful local SEO ranking factors, and they directly influence whether a potential customer chooses you over a competitor. Businesses with a higher volume of positive reviews consistently outrank those with few or no reviews in local search results.

How to generate more reviews:

  • Simply ask satisfied customers — most people are happy to leave a review if you make it easy.
  • Send a follow-up email or text with a direct link to your Google review page.
  • Add a "Leave Us a Review" button or link to your website.
  • Train your staff to mention reviews after a positive interaction.

Just as important as getting reviews is responding to them — both positive and negative. Thanking happy customers shows appreciation, while professionally addressing negative feedback demonstrates that you take customer satisfaction seriously. Google rewards businesses that actively engage with their reviews.

Optimize Your Website for Local Keywords

Your website is the foundation of your entire local SEO strategy. If it's not optimized properly, even a great Google Business Profile can only take you so far. Here's what you need to focus on:

  • Include location-specific keywords throughout your website — in your page titles, headings, meta descriptions, and body content. For example, instead of just "web design services," use "web design services in Richmond, VA."
  • Create a dedicated location page if you serve multiple cities or neighborhoods. Each page should be unique and genuinely helpful to visitors in that area.
  • Add your Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) in a consistent format on every page, ideally in the footer.
  • Embed a Google Map on your contact page to reinforce your geographic location to search engines.
  • Write locally relevant blog content that answers questions your target customers are actually searching for — like this very article.

A fast, mobile-friendly, well-structured website also signals credibility to Google and keeps visitors engaged longer — both of which improve your search rankings.

Build Local Citations and Backlinks

A local citation is any online mention of your business's name, address, and phone number. Citations appear on business directories, review sites, and social platforms — and they help search engines verify that your business is legitimate and where it says it is.

Start by listing your business on the most authoritative directories:

  • Yelp
  • Bing Places
  • Apple Maps
  • Facebook Business
  • Better Business Bureau (BBB)
  • Industry-specific directories relevant to your niche

Make sure your business information is consistent across every platform. Even small discrepancies — like abbreviating "Street" as "St." on one site and spelling it out on another — can confuse search engines and hurt your rankings.

Beyond citations, earning local backlinks (links from other reputable websites in your area) gives your site significant authority. Sponsor local events, partner with neighboring businesses, contribute guest articles to local blogs, or join your local Chamber of Commerce — all of these can earn you valuable backlinks that boost your local search visibility.

Track Your Results and Keep Improving

Local SEO isn't a one-and-done effort — it's an ongoing process that rewards consistency. Use free tools like Google Analytics and Google Search Console to track how much traffic your website is receiving from organic search, which keywords are driving visitors, and what pages they're landing on.

Monitor your Google Business Profile insights to see how customers are finding you — whether through direct searches, discovery searches, or maps. Track your ranking positions for your most important local keywords using tools like BrightLocal or Semrush.

Use this data to continuously refine your strategy. Double down on what's working, fix what isn't, and stay ahead of your local competition by publishing fresh content, gathering new reviews, and keeping your business information up to date.
