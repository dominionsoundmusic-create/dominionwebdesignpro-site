Facts and sources
- Chicago has 77 community areas, defined in the 1920s by University of Chicago researchers to compare census data; only two boundary changes since (O'Hare added, Edgewater split from Uptown); no officially recognized neighborhoods. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_areas_of_Chicago and https://iecam.illinois.edu/data-descriptions/geographic-region-chicago-community-areas-ccas
- Population roughly 2.7 million (2024 estimate 2,721,308 per NBC; Tribune "just over 2.7 million"). https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/what-are-illinois-10-largest-cities-what-to-know-after-recent-census-estimates/3748482/?amp=1
- SSAs: local property tax districts for services beyond city services; DPD contracts nonprofits (typically chambers or business associations); mayor-appointed commission of property owners and tenants sets budget annually; services include maintenance/beautification, marketing, events, security, facades; city lists 55 active SSAs. https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/dcd/supp_info/special_service_areassaprogram.html
- 26th Street, Little Village: 2-mile stretch Western to Pulaski, second-highest-grossing shopping district after the Magnificent Mile; about $900 million in 2015 sales (Bisnow). https://www.bisnow.com/news/chicago/commercial-real-estate/4-reasons-not-to-ignore-little-village-75313 , https://news.wttw.com/2015/10/07/little-village-s-26th-street-corridor-next-mag-mile
- Little Village Chamber is a BACP NBDC with services in Spanish and English. https://littlevillagechamber.org/wp-content/uploads/BACP_NBDC_LVCC-2023-Webpage-Content-1.pdf
- Devon Avenue Little India: grew from early 1970s; first Indian store India Sari Palace 1972; Patel Brothers 1974; runs through West Ridge, Rogers Park, Edgewater; restaurants, sweet shops, grocers, sari and jewelry shops; people from many countries. https://www.wbez.org/curious-city/2022/11/17/how-did-devon-avenue-become-chicagos-little-india , https://abc7chicago.com/chicago-little-india-devon-avenue-avenue-indian-food/5649482/
- Milwaukee Avenue: diagonal former Northwest Plank Road; passes Wicker Park, Logan Square, Avondale; Diversey to Addison stretch still called the Polish Village. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee_Avenue_(Chicago) , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Chicago
- Andersonville Chamber supports more than 400 local and independent businesses near and along Clark Street. https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1362818645
- Chinatown around Wentworth and Cermak: restaurants, bakeries, grocers, gift shops; over 400 businesses (WTTW). https://www.wttw.com/southside/near-southwest/chinatown
- NBDCs: BACP community partners giving no-cost hyper-local help, licensing and public way permits. https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/bacp/nbdc/nbdcflyer06172025.pdf
- BACP Small Business Center (licensing) at City Hall, 121 N. LaSalle. https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/dps/Outreach/ResourcesStartRunGrowBusiness.pdf (document is circa 2020; room number and walk-in policy deliberately left out)
- Illinois SBDC hosts: Chicagoland Chamber (https://www.chicagolandchamber.org/chamber-news/sbdc-celebrates-one-year-anniversary/), Polsky Exchange on 53rd Street (https://polsky.uchicago.edu/local-business-owners/small-business-development-center/), Chinese Mutual Aid Association on Argyle Street, multilingual (https://chinesemutualaid.org/_files/ugd/197c53_49f04a3c5c46402abe115b13ec198193.pdf).
