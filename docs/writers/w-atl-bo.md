## Researched facts and sources
1. Census ABS (2024 ABS, reference year 2023): about 201,000 Black or African American-owned employer businesses,
   3.4% of employer businesses, receipts $249.0 billion; about 5.9 million employer firms total.
   https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025/business-owner-characteristics.html (Nov 20, 2025)
2. Same release, Nonemployer Statistics by Demographics 2023: about 4.4 million Black or African American-owned
   nonemployer businesses (14.4%), receipts $128.7 billion. Same URL.
3. Black alone share of U.S. population about 13.5% (QuickFacts, V2025 estimates).
   https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/US/RHI225225
4. Fed SBCS 2021 Report on Firms Owned by People of Color (survey fielded late 2020): 13% of Black-owned firms that
   sought financing received all of it vs 40% of white-owned firms.
   https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/news/regional_outreach/2021/20210415 and
   https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/-/media/project/smallbizcredittenant/fedsmallbusinesssite/fedsmallbusiness/files/2021/sbcs-report-on-firms-owned-by-people-of-color
   Fact-checker: confirm the 13%/40% pair (page says the report was "published in 2021"; the fielding date was not stated because it was not seen directly).
5. NMSDC MBE definition: for-profit, at least 51% owned, operated and controlled by minority group members; groups
   Asian-Indian, Asian-Pacific, Black, Hispanic, Native American; owners U.S. citizens; active in daily management;
   review includes documents, interviews and a site visit; certification does not promise contracts.
   https://nmsdc.org/certifications/definition-of-an-mbe/ and https://nmsdc.org/certifications/certification-process/
6. Google Business Profile Black-owned identity attribute is opt-in / self-identified by the business, and the
   owner can turn it on or off; also shown on Shopping.
   https://support.google.com/business/answer/14150971?hl=en
7. ICANN: the registered name holder (registrant) is responsible for the domain and must keep its contact details accurate.
   https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/benefits-2013-09-16-en
