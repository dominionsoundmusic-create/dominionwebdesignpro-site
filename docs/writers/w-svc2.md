## /web-design-for-plumbers/
Target: web design for plumbers (720/mo, SD 16).
Facts stated and sources:
- 22 TAC 367.10: RMP of record and plumbing company owner must ensure advertisements to solicit plumbing business show the
  RMP's license number regardless of media; examples business cards, flyers, handouts.
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/texas/22-Tex-Admin-Code-SS-367-10
  (Websites are not named in the rule; the page says "a website that advertises your services is advertising, so the safe
  practice is to show the number" and points to the Board for certainty.)
- TSBPE public license search shows status, expiration, disciplinary actions; TSBPE consumer page tells homeowners the written
  estimate and invoice should carry the RMP license number.
  https://tsbpe.texas.gov/consumer-information/ ; https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/TSBPE_HowTo_PublicLicenseSearch.pdf
- Google Business Profile guidelines: service-area businesses should hide their address (plumber at home is Google's example);
  service area should not extend beyond about 2 hours driving from the base. https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
Left out: the Board penalty amount ($5,000 Class A for failing to display license number) found only in a 2017 penalty schedule PDF; not stated.

## /roofing-contractor-website-design/
Target: roofing contractor website design (50/mo, SD 35).
Facts stated and sources (TDI page: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/roofing-and-insurance-know-the-law.html):
- Illegal in Texas for a contractor to offer to waive, rebate or absorb a policyholder's deductible (also Bus. & Com. Code 27.02,
  https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.27.htm).
- Contractor may not act as a public adjuster or advertise to adjust claims on property where it provides or may provide
  contracting services (Ins. Code 4102.163, https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/IN/htm/IN.4102.htm).
- Texas roofing contracts of $1,000 or more paid by insurance must include a notice that the policyholder must pay the deductible.
Deliberately NOT stated: "Texas has no statewide roofing license." Search results conflict: several 2026 licensing guides say
no state license; one says HB 3344 (89th Leg.) creates a TDLR reroofing contractor license with rules due April 1, 2026 and
licenses from June 1, 2026. I could not confirm enactment from an official source, so the page says only that roofing rules
vary and "in Texas they have been changing" and tells roofers to show whatever license or registration they hold.
Fact-checkers: please confirm HB 3344 status on capitol.texas.gov / tdlr.texas.gov; if enacted, the page can name it.

## /web-design-for-law-firms/
Target: web design for law firms (2,900/mo). Supporting: how much does a law firm website cost (FAQ).
Facts stated and sources:
- ABA Model Rule 7.1: no false or misleading communication about the lawyer or the lawyer's services; misleading includes
  material misrepresentation or omission; comments cover unjustified expectations. Rule listed in the ABA Model Rules ToC
  (https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/model_rules_of_professional_conduct_table_of_contents/);
  text verified via state adoptions (e.g. Colorado, D.C.) in search results, not the ABA page itself.
- ABA Model Rules limit claims of specialization (Model Rule 7.2(c)); stated generally, no rule number on the page.
- Texas: Rules 7.01 to 7.03 content; Rule 7.04 filing with the Advertising Review Committee within 10 days of dissemination;
  Rule 7.05(b) exempts law firm website content except the homepage unless otherwise exempt; whole site must comply.
  https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/filing-requirements-for-advertisements-and-solicitation-communications/
  https://www.legalethicstexas.com/resources/rules/texas-disciplinary-rules-of-professional-conduct/communications-exempt-from-filing-requirements/
  https://www.legalethicstexas.com/ethics-question-of-the-month/ethics-question-of-the-month-july-2025/
- ABA Formal Opinion 10-457 (2010, Lawyer Websites): listing contact info generally does not start a prospective-client
  relationship; specifically inviting submission of information about a possible representation can; possible Rule 1.18 conflicts.
  Verified through secondary coverage (ezel.ai summary, Minnesota Lawyer, NY Daily Record), not the opinion PDF.
Left out: Texas "name of responsible lawyer" requirement (only older rule text found; current numbering unconfirmed), Texas
specialization rules by number, any cost-of-law-firm-website market figures.
