Which package fits which business

A web design quote should start with how your customers find you, not with how many pages a designer wants to sell. Here is the honest version.

Starter fits

A business that mostly serves one town, or one that is brand new and needs a real website quickly. A barber in Big Sandy, a bookkeeper working from home, a food truck with a regular corner. Five pages, written well, are enough, and buying more would be waste.

Pro fits

A business whose customers come from several towns and describe the work in mostly the same words. A cleaning company covering a county, a salon that pulls clients from the next three towns over. Ten city pages put a real answer in front of each of those towns.

Agency fits

A business with several services across a wide area, where customers type genuinely different things: a remodeler, a law office with multiple practice areas, a clinic with a long list of treatments. Keyword research decides the page list first.
