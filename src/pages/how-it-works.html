A web design process that starts with your site, not an invoice

The usual web design process goes deposit, contract, a few weeks of quiet, then a reveal you are too far in to argue with. Ours runs the other direction. You see a demo of your website first. If it looks like your business, you pick a package. If it does not, you have lost a minute.

From there, one person does the work. Maurice Johnson writes the pages, builds the site and launches it, and he is the person you can reach when you have a question. Below is every step, what it costs (most cost nothing), and what he will need from you.

Each step in detail

  1. You ask for the demoGo to the demo page, type in your business name and what you do, and a preview site is built for your business. If you would rather talk, call (903) 636-7511. The AI receptionist answers any hour, and if you ask about a website preview it transfers you to Maurice.
  2. You look at it where your customers willOpen the link on your phone, because that is where most of your customers will see the real thing. Show it to whoever you trust. Note what feels wrong. A demo is a starting point, and its job is to show you the direction before money changes hands.
  3. You pick a package, or you do notStarter is $497, Pro is $997 and Agency is $1,997, all one time. The buttons on the pricing page go to secure Stripe checkout. If none of it is right, you owe nothing and nobody will chase you.
  4. You send the detailsFill in the client intake form. It asks for everything Maurice needs to write your pages, so there is not a week of back-and-forth emails. The list of what to have ready is below.
  5. Maurice writes and builds every pageEach page is written by hand for your business and your towns. On Pro, each of the 10 city pages starts with research into that town. On Agency, the work starts with keyword research across your whole service area, which decides the page list.
  6. You review and approveYou get a link to the working site before launch. Read every page. If a service is described wrong or a town is missing, say so and it gets fixed.
  7. LaunchThe site goes live on your domain. Page titles, descriptions and the sitemap are submitted to Google. On Pro and Agency the schema markup and Google Business Profile setup are done as part of launch.

What Maurice needs from you

The intake form collects most of this. Having it ready before you start is the single biggest thing you can do to speed up the web design process.

  • Business name, phone, email and hours, exactly as you want them shown
  • Every service you offer, in the words your customers use for it
  • The towns you serve, starting with the ones that matter most
  • Real photos of your work, your team or your shop, if you have them (phone photos are fine)
  • Your logo, if you have one
  • Your Facebook and Instagram links
  • Where your domain is registered, if you already own one
  • Anything a customer always asks you on the phone

No photos? That is common. Maurice will tell you exactly what to shoot with your phone, and real pictures of your own work are worth more than stock images.

What happens after launch

The site is yours

You own it outright. You are not renting pages that disappear if you stop paying a monthly bill.

Upkeep

Hosting, maintenance and updates are included while you keep the AI agent. Without the agent they are $49 a month.

Changes without a ticket system

Prices change, hours change, you add a service. While your upkeep is active (included with the AI agent, or $49 a month), you send a message and it gets done.

Search takes its own time

Submitting a sitemap tells Google your pages exist, but Google itself calls a sitemap a hint and decides when to crawl. Nobody can promise a ranking or a date, and we do not.
