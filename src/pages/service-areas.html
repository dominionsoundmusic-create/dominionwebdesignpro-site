How a Texas web designer in Big Sandy covers a state this size

Texas is too big for anyone to be local everywhere, and a web designer who claims an office in every metro is usually renting a mailbox. Dominion Web Design Pro is one Texas web designer, Maurice Johnson, working out of Big Sandy in Upshur County. He writes and builds every page himself and delivers the finished site by link, phone and email.

That setup decides how the service area works. East Texas is close enough to drive. The big Texas metros, where business owners search hardest for web design, each get a city page researched for that market. Metro Atlanta has its own set of researched pages. And a business anywhere else in the country can still order a site, because nothing about building one requires being in the same room.

Texas has roughly 3.5 million small businesses, second only to California, according to the U.S. Small Business Administration's Office of Advocacy. Many of them are owner-run shops, crews and offices that depend on nearby customers finding them. That is the business this site is built for.

Why a website company in Texas should write for your town, not for "Texas"

Searches like "web design tx" and "website design company texas" are typed by people at the start of the hunt. By the time a homeowner in Katy or a restaurant manager in Waco is looking for a business, they search for the service and the town. A site that only says "serving all of Texas" gives Google nothing to match against that second search.

So the pages a Texas business needs are specific: a page for each service, and a page for each town that brings in work, each one saying something true about that place. That is the same method used on this site. It is also why page count is never the selling point here. We do not sell page counts; a few dozen pages that each earn their place beat a pile of copies with the city name swapped.
