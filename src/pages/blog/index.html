How each post is put together

Every post starts with research, not a template. Prices come from the companies that set them or from comparison sites that show when they checked. Rules come from the organization that writes them, such as ICANN for domain names or Google for search. When something cannot be verified, it stays out.

Each post also says what we sell where it is relevant, with the real prices, so you never have to guess. A Dominion website is a one-time price of $497, $997 or $1,997, and you can see a free demo before you pay anything.
