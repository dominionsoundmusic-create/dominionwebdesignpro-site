Web design and web development: what is the difference?

People use the two terms as if they meant the same thing, and on a small business site they mostly overlap. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics covers them in one occupational profile but splits the work in two.

Web design

The part a visitor experiences: page layout, navigation, and how easily someone can find what they came for. A designer's question is "can a customer on a phone find the phone number in two seconds?"

Web development

The part underneath: building and maintaining the site in code, and technical matters like how fast it loads and how much traffic it can take. A developer's question is "will this page load on one bar of signal?"

A large company might hire separate people for each. A local business does not need to. Maurice Johnson does the design, the writing and the build, which is one reason the site comes out consistent from page to page.

Search, Google and getting found

Is SEO included?

The basics are in every package: page titles, descriptions and a sitemap submitted to Google. Pro adds schema markup and Google Business Profile setup. Agency adds keyword research across your service area. More on SEO web design.

Why does my site need to work well on a phone?

Because Google now crawls and indexes sites primarily as a phone sees them. Google announced in October 2023 that its move to mobile-first indexing was complete. Every site here is designed for the phone first.

What are city pages, and do I need them?

A city page is one page about one service, written for one town. Whether you need ten or fifty depends on how many towns you serve and how many different ways customers describe your work. How city pages work explains the math.

Ownership, domains and leaving

Who owns the website?

You do, outright, once it is paid for.

Who owns the domain name?

You should. ICANN calls the person or company that holds the rights to a domain the registrant. If a web designer registers your domain with their own details, they may be listed as the registrant instead of you, which is how owners get stuck. You can check yours with ICANN's free lookup tool.

What if I want to leave later?

The site is yours. Monthly services can be cancelled, and you are not locked into anything by the build itself.
