Someone actually builds your website

When people search for a local web designer near me, most are not looking for a zip code. They want a person they can reach, who will look at their business and build something that fits it. That is what Dominion Web Design Pro is. It is run out of Big Sandy in East Texas by Maurice Johnson, and the person who builds your site is the person you talk to about it. Dominion Web Design Pro, a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group, is a Black-owned business; if that matters in your choice of web developer, here is what it means for your project and how to check any company's ownership.

Every page is written by hand for that business and the towns it serves. Nothing is generated from a list of city names and dropped into a template. That is slower, and it is the reason the pages read like someone looked at your business.

We build it before you pay for it

Most web design goes: deposit, contract, three weeks of silence, then a reveal you are too far in to argue with. We do the opposite. Tell us about your business and a demo site is built for it. You get a link and look at a website for your business on your own phone.

If it is wrong, it gets changed. If you do not want it, you have lost nothing but the time it took to look. Nobody should have to pay a stranger on the internet for something they have not seen.

What it costs

Three packages, each a one-time price, and the site is yours to keep. No setup fee, no contract, no early termination clause.

  • Starter, $497A 5-page website written by hand, live in 7 to 14 days.
  • Pro, $99710 pages, 10 researched city pages, schema and Google Business Profile setup, plus an AI chat widget that is free for 14 days, then $297 a month.
  • Agency, $1,997Keyword research across your service area and a page for every town-and-service combination worth having, usually 30 to 60, plus AI phone agent setup. The agent is free for 7 days, then $297 a month for up to 750 minutes, cancel any time.

Hosting, maintenance and updates are included while you keep the AI agent. If you decide you do not want the agent, they are $49 a month. Work outside the packages costs more, and you will be told the number before anything starts. The pricing page has the full comparison.

What you actually get

A site built for a phone

Many of the people who find you will do it on a phone, often on a weak signal. That is what the site is designed for first, then checked on a desktop afterwards.

Local search set up properly

Service areas named, the towns you actually cover written into the pages, titles, descriptions and a sitemap submitted to Google. Included, not sold back to you later.

Your own photos

Real pictures of your work beat stock images. If you have none, you will be told exactly what to shoot on your phone.

Changes without a ticket system

Prices change, hours change, you add a service. While your upkeep is active, you send a message and it gets done. You are not filing a support request.

Local where it counts, remote where it does not

Big Sandy sits in Upshur County on U.S. Highway 80, about 14 miles southwest of Gilmer and within an easy drive of Tyler and Longview. If your business is in that part of East Texas, Maurice is close by, and a call can be put through to him to talk it through.

Everywhere else, websites are built and delivered remotely, so where you are makes little difference to the job. You see the demo by link, approve pages by email and call when you have a question. There is no office anywhere except Big Sandy, and no page on this site will pretend otherwise.

Being nearby does help in one way: knowing the towns. Every city page on this site was researched before it was written, and the city directory and Texas service areas show where that research has gone so far.
