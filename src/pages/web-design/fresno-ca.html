The biggest farm county in the country, by the numbers

Fresno County's 2024 crop and livestock report put the county's gross agricultural production at $9,029,122,000, a record, and that total made it the top farm county in the United States again after Kern County held the title for 2023. Almonds led at about $1.46 billion, grapes came second at about $1.04 billion and pistachios third at about $860 million. The Board of Supervisors accepted the report on November 4, 2025.

Those are farm-gate values, not profits, but they explain the business directory. Behind every orchard sit the people who prune it, spray it, harvest it, cool the fruit, pack it, haul it, insure it, fix the equipment and keep the books. Most of those firms are small, and many of them are still found mainly by word of mouth, which works right up until a new grower moves in or an old customer retires.

None of these buyers wants to read a slogan. They want a fact that tells them you can handle their job. Maurice asks what those facts are before he writes a line.

Fresno web design for a city of many languages

Census QuickFacts counts Fresno at 542,107 people in the 2020 Census, with 50.6% Hispanic or Latino and 14.2% Asian in its 2019 to 2023 estimates. The Pew Research Center's analysis of 2017 to 2019 survey data put the Fresno metro's Hmong population at about 35,000, the second largest of any metro area after Minneapolis and St. Paul. A University of California Cooperative Extension advisor has reported more than 900 Hmong farmers among over 1,300 Southeast Asian farms in Fresno County, based on a 2007 survey, many of them growing Asian specialty crops for farmers markets and restaurants.

Places Fresno customers type into the search bar

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Distance is fixed, but a site can make it plain where you are and how far you travel, and the names people actually use help with that.

Tower District

Centered on Olive and Wishon avenues and named for the Tower Theatre, it is the city's arts and nightlife pocket, with coffee shops, vintage stores and breweries in older storefronts.

Fulton Street downtown

The six-block Fulton Mall reopened to cars in October 2017, for the first time in more than 50 years, with two-way traffic, parking and wide sidewalks. Downtown owners there have spent years filling empty storefronts.

Fig Garden Village and River Park

Fig Garden Village sits at Palm and Shaw avenues near Old Fig Garden; River Park is the north Fresno shopping village near Highway 41. Salons, clinics and boutiques here draw from the northern neighborhoods.

Clovis next door

Clovis is its own city on Fresno's northeast side, and its residents often search by that name. If you serve both, each deserves an honest section rather than one line that says "Fresno area."

One example owner, walked through

Picture a two-truck mobile repair business that fixes tractors, sprayers and forklifts on farms south and west of town. Today its customers call the owner's cell, and new ones find him only when a neighbor passes the number along. Here is how Maurice would build that site.

  • A home page that leads with the field call. "We come to you" in the first line, the counties covered, the machine brands handled and a tap-to-call button.
  • A service page for each kind of machine. Tractors, sprayers, forklifts and packing line equipment each get a short page, because that is how people search.
  • A plain harvest-season note. Expected response times when every grower needs help the same week, so nobody is surprised.
  • Photos from his own phone. His trucks, his shop and real jobs, never stock pictures of somebody else's equipment.
