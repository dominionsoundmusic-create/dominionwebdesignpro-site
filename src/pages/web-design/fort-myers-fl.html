Web design Fort Myers owners can count on has to handle two very different customers. One flew in from Boston or Minneapolis for a week of spring training and is searching for breakfast near the ballpark. The other lives on a Cape Coral canal and needs a boat lift fixed before the weekend. Neither will scroll far on a phone to find out whether you can help.

Dominion Web Design Pro is Maurice Johnson, who writes every page by hand, builds a free demo of your own site before you spend a dollar, and charges a printed one-time price.

Web design Fort Myers Florida needs for February and March

Southwest Florida marked 100 years of hosting Major League spring training in 2025, and Fort Myers has two teams. The Boston Red Sox have trained at JetBlue Park since 2012, on a field built to Fenway Park's dimensions with its own Green Monster and about 11,000 seats. The Minnesota Twins play at Hammond Stadium in the Lee Health Sports Complex, which Lee County lists at 9,300 seats. The two parks are a short drive apart.

The money is real and measured. Lee County's most recent study of the spring training season, from 2018, found non-resident visitors spent $68.9 million during their trips, $56.7 million of it outside the ballparks. Forty-one percent of the visitors surveyed said baseball was their only reason for coming.

Spending outside the parks lands at restaurants, vacation rentals, shops and services around town, and plenty of those are small and locally owned. A visitor deciding between two of them on a phone has a short list of questions: how far from the park, open before or after the game, can I book online or call, and what does it cost.

A site that answers those four on the first screen wins the booking. One that opens with a slideshow and a paragraph about passion loses it to the place across the street.

Fort Myers website design for the Cape Coral canal trades

Across the Caloosahatchee, Cape Coral says it has more than 400 miles of navigable waterways. With well over 200,000 residents in recent Census Bureau estimates, Cape Coral is the largest city in Lee County, roughly twice the size of Fort Myers.

All that water keeps a whole set of trades busy: dock builders, seawall crews, boat lift installers and repairers, marine mechanics, mobile detailers, canal-front pool and lawn services. Their customers are specific about what they need, so the website has to be specific back.

What sizes of boat or lift do you handle?A short specs list on the lift or dock page
Who handles the permit paperwork?A plain answer on the seawall and dock pages, with no promises about approval times

How fast can someone look at it?Honest response times and a tap-to-call button near the top

Responsive web design Fort Myers customers can use on a dock

Responsive web design means the same site reshapes itself for whatever screen opens it. On a phone the menu folds away, text stays readable without pinching, and the call button sits where a thumb can reach it. On a laptop the same pages spread into columns. There is one site to update, not a desktop version and a mobile version that drift apart.

Places Fort Myers customers name when they search

  • The River DistrictDowntown along the Caloosahatchee. On the first Friday evening of each month a free Art Walk opens galleries and shops downtown. Shops there should post their Art Walk hours.
  • McGregor BoulevardHome of the Edison and Ford Winter Estates at 2350 McGregor, more than 20 acres on the river. Mina Edison gave the Edison property to the city in 1947, and today the estates draw more than 200,000 visitors a year.
  • EsteroTo the south, an unincorporated community until it became a village on December 31, 2014; the 2020 Census counted 36,939 residents.
  • Lehigh Acres and Cape CoralEast of the city and across the river. People there search by those names, not by "Fort Myers," so a company that serves them needs pages that say so.

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. A page written about Estero, for a landscaper who really works there, gives Google a reason to match that business to an Estero search. Prominence grows with reviews and time, and no honest designer will promise a ranking.
