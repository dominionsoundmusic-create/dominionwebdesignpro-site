Web design Shreveport businesses get real value from starts with the river. Shreveport sits on one bank of the Red River and Bossier City on the other, two cities in two parishes that most people treat as one place, and the customers who keep both going come from a wide circle: Caddo and Bossier parishes, East Texas, south Arkansas. A site that only says "Shreveport" leaves out half the people who would drive to you.

Dominion Web Design Pro is Maurice Johnson, building sites by hand in Big Sandy, Texas, on the other side of the state line. You see a free demo first and pay one printed price.

Web design Shreveport LA owners need when the city shrinks but the region holds steady

A KEEL News analysis of Census estimates put Shreveport at about 175,900 people in July 2025, down from about 187,500 in April 2020. Caddo Parish lost about 1,100 residents between 2024 and 2025, the smallest one-year drop since 2020. The whole Shreveport-Bossier City metro, at about 383,000 people, barely changed.

For a small business, that means growth rarely comes from a flood of new neighbors. It comes from winning more of the customers already in the region, including the ones on the far side of the river or the state line. Those people pick between you and a competitor by what they see on a phone, so the site has to answer the basics fast: where you are, which towns you serve, when you are open and how to reach you.

Corridors people name when they give directions

Ask a local where a business is and you will hear a street or a neighborhood before you hear a ZIP code. Highland is a historic neighborhood known for its architecture, parks and restaurants. Line Avenue runs through Highland and South Highlands and is lined with shops, places to eat and antique stores. Youree Drive, farther southeast, is a major retail road lined with shopping centers.

Across the river, East Texas Street, part of U.S. 80 through Bossier City, carries a lot of history. From the 1940s to the 1970s it was the Bossier Strip, an entertainment district of nightclubs, bars and restaurants that drew airmen from Barksdale, and its heyday ended long before riverboat casinos arrived on the riverfront in the 1990s. Today it is a commercial road.

Why should that shape your website? Google says local results depend mostly on relevance, distance and prominence. Someone searching "lawyer near Line Avenue" or "tire shop Bossier" is giving Google a place to measure distance from. If your pages name the corridor you sit on and the areas you cover, you are not asking Google to guess.

A law office near downtown, a clinic off Youree and a contractor in Bossier each need a different page plan. We write it around where your customers really come from, and nobody can promise a ranking, so we will not.

Free help on both sides of the Red River

LSBDC Northwest Louisiana

The Louisiana Small Business Development Center office at Southern University Shreveport, on Hilry Huckaby Avenue, gives no-cost, confidential consulting to owners across northwest Louisiana, including Caddo, Bossier, De Soto, Red River, Bienville and Webster parishes.

Greater Shreveport Chamber

Founded in 1910 and based on Edwards Street downtown. It lists small business resources and an APEX Accelerator for owners who want government contracts, and has reported more than 1,100 member companies.

Bossier Chamber of Commerce

On Benton Road in Bossier City. Volunteer diplomats call or visit member businesses, and the chamber points owners to workforce programs, including SkillBridge for service members leaving the military.

We are not affiliated with any of them. Each is a good first call for a new or growing business.
