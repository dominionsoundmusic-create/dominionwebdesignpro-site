Why Decatur web design starts with the Square

The Square around the Old DeKalb County Courthouse is the center of gravity. The MARTA Blue Line stops at Decatur station a short walk away, and festivals such as the Decatur Arts Festival and the Decatur Book Festival fill the streets with people who did not plan where they would eat or shop. Many of them pull out a phone, type a search like "coffee near Decatur Square" or "bookstore Decatur GA," and choose from whatever the map shows them in the next ten seconds.

That is a narrow window, and a website either fits through it or does not. Hours have to be current on the site and on Google. The menu or product list should load as real text, not a scanned flyer. A visitor coming off the train should see at a glance how far you are from the Square and whether you take walk-ins. None of that is fancy, and it is easy for a shop site to miss at least one piece of it.

Oakhurst Village, East Lake and the rest of the city

Decatur is more than its courthouse blocks. Oakhurst, the historic neighborhood on the southwest side of the city, has its own small commercial center in Oakhurst Village, with restaurants and shops a few minutes from downtown. The East Lake MARTA station sits at Oakhurst's northern edge, so a business there can be reached by train as easily as one on the Square. Agnes Scott College adds students, faculty and visiting families to the customer base.

For a business owner, each pocket of the city behaves like its own small market. Someone in Oakhurst searching for a lunch spot rarely wants to cross town for it. Name the neighborhood you are in, the nearest station and the street you face, and your site starts answering the question the searcher actually asked.

  • Your street address, written the same way on the site, on Google and on every directory listing.
  • The neighborhood by name: the Square, Oakhurst Village or wherever you really are.
  • Directions for people arriving on foot or by MARTA, not only by car.
  • Hours that change for festival weekends and holidays, updated before the weekend, not after.

"Decatur" is not only in Georgia

Here is a problem specific to this city's name. There is a Decatur in Alabama, one in Illinois and one in North Texas, along with several smaller ones. If your site says only "Decatur" in its titles and contact details, search engines have to guess which one you mean, and an out-of-state visitor clicking through will leave the second they see the wrong state.

The fix costs nothing and is easy to forget. Say "Decatur, Georgia" or "Decatur, GA" in the page title, the opening paragraph and the footer. Mention DeKalb County. Put your full address in LocalBusiness schema markup, which the Pro package includes. These are small edits, but they are the difference between a page that clearly belongs to this Decatur and one that competes with three other cities for the same word.
