What an independent agent's website has to prove

When someone in Urbandale is shopping for home and auto coverage, they are comparing you with a dozen other agents and with a carrier's own website. The agent who wins the call is often the one whose site answers the quiet questions: Are you licensed in Iowa? Which companies can you quote? Will a real person help me when I file a claim? Checklist for a Des Moines insurance or advisory site

Use it to judge your current site, ours or anyone's.

  • Your Iowa license or registration stated accurately, with the lines of coverage you are authorized to write.
  • The carriers you are appointed with, only if each one allows it, and no logos you do not have permission to use.
  • What happens after a loss: who answers, when, and the number to call, separate from the sales line.
  • The towns you serve by name, from Des Moines out to Ankeny, Waukee, Johnston or Indianola, with an office address that matches your Google Business Profile.
  • A short, honest about page with your real name and background, because people buy coverage from a person.
  • A quote request form that emails you every lead, with only the questions you truly need answered up front.

The same list works with small changes for financial planners, tax preparers, adjusters and the auto body and restoration shops that see a lot of insurance work.

Eleven days in August

The other big date on the Des Moines calendar is the Iowa State Fair. The 2026 fair ran August 13 to 23 and drew 1,218,834 people, an all-time record, beating the 1,182,682 set in 2024 and well above the 1,160,121 who came in 2025.

For a food vendor, a craft maker or a small brand selling at the fair, those eleven days can be a big share of the year. Plenty of fairgoers will meet you there, love what you sell and then search your name a week later from home. If all they find is an old social media post, the sale ends there. A simple site with what you make, where to buy it the rest of the year, catering or wholesale details and a way to get in touch keeps that customer.
