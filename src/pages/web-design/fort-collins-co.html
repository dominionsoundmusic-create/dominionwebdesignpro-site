Fort Collins website design starts with the place names

The Census Bureau estimated the city's population at 170,376 in 2023, and customers here describe location by the stretch of town they know. A page that uses those names gives searchers and Google something concrete.

Old Town

The historic downtown, packed with independent shops, restaurants and bars. The City of Fort Collins history site says Disneyland's Main Street U.S.A. drew on Old Town, based on the account of Harper Goff, a Disney designer who grew up here and photographed its buildings. Fort Collins is also known for its craft breweries. Old Town sites need hours, a map and photos that match the storefront.

Campus West and Midtown on College Avenue

Student-heavy blocks west of campus, and the long commercial run of College Avenue through Midtown. Customers here are often new to town each fall, so they search by category and by distance, not by name.

Harmony Road and south Fort Collins

Shopping centers, medical offices and newer neighborhoods. Service businesses here draw from both Fort Collins and the towns to the south and east.

Loveland, Windsor and Timnath

Many trades and home services work all of these. A short, honest paragraph about each town you serve beats a list of twenty suburb names pasted in the footer.
