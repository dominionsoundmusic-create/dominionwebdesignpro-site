Website design in Las Vegas, Nevada starts with knowing which Las Vegas

Here is a fact that surprises newcomers: most of the Strip is not in the city of Las Vegas. It sits in the unincorporated towns of Paradise and Winchester, governed by Clark County. Paradise was created as a town in 1950, after casino operators pushed for town status to head off annexation by the city.

Where people live is just as split. Clark County's 2024 place estimates count about 681,000 people in the city of Las Vegas and about 355,000 in Henderson, out of roughly 2.42 million countywide. Two of the biggest communities, Spring Valley and Enterprise, are unincorporated towns that each passed 215,000 residents in the 2020 census. Summerlin, the large planned community on the west side, sits partly inside city limits and partly in the county. North Las Vegas is its own city again.

For a business, the point is simple. A customer in Enterprise might never type "Las Vegas" into a search box. They type "mechanic in Spring Valley" or "salon in Henderson." Google says its local results weigh relevance, distance and prominence, and relevance means how well a business matches what was searched. A site that only ever says "serving Las Vegas" leaves a lot of those searches unanswered. Your pages should name the communities you really serve, in the words residents use for them.

One mailing address, six licensing offices

The same patchwork shows up when you get licensed. Most Nevada businesses need a State Business License from the Secretary of State, filed through SilverFlume, and the few that qualify for an exemption still have to file for it. The local license then depends on where the business physically sits. Clark County notes that it holds six jurisdictions, each with its own licensing agency, and that a large part of the valley has a Las Vegas mailing address while actually lying in unincorporated Clark County. The county's jurisdiction locator settles which one applies, and some businesses that work across lines need more than one license.

A website does not replace any of that paperwork, but it can make the result easy to see. If you are licensed, put the license details on your contact page. If you work in the county and in Henderson, say so in plain words. A resident comparing three contractors notices which one is clear about it.

The local businesses this Las Vegas website design work is for

Nevada is a small business state. The SBA Office of Advocacy's 2024 profile counted 333,471 small businesses in Nevada, 99.3% of all businesses in the state, employing 45.3% of its workforce. Most are not on the Strip. They fix cars, cut hair, clean homes, watch children and repair air conditioners for the people who live here.

Auto repair and detailing

A driver whose check engine light comes on during a commute wants three things from a shop's site: the services you handle, whether you can see them today, and how to get there. List the makes you work on, your hours, and the cross streets. Photos of your actual bays beat stock pictures of sports cars.

Salons, barbers and nail studios

Clients choose by stylist and by photo. Give each chair or specialty its own section, show real work, and make booking a single tap. If you take walk-ins, say when.

House cleaning and home services

Cleaners, plumbers, pest control and pool crews go to the customer, so the site carries the job of naming the areas covered. A page for Henderson and one for the northwest valley, each written for that area, reads very differently from a single line of town names.

Child care and family services

Parents read slowly and carefully. Hours, ages accepted, licensing, the location and a way to schedule a visit matter far more than design flourishes.

A valley that does not keep office hours

If your customers include people who work evening or overnight shifts, they may be looking for a mechanic or a stylist at 2 a.m., long after you have locked up. A website is the one employee that is always on. At minimum it should let a late-night visitor send a request that lands in your inbox, with a clear promise about when you will reply.
