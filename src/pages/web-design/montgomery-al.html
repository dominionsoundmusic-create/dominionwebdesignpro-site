Web design Montgomery AL visitors can use on museum day

Montgomery's history draws travelers on purpose. The Equal Justice Initiative opened the Legacy Museum and the National Memorial for Peace and Justice on April 26, 2018; the memorial hangs 800 steel columns, one for each county where racial terror lynchings took place. The Rosa Parks Museum, run by Troy University, opened on December 1, 2000, near the stop where Parks was arrested in 1955. The Civil Rights Memorial, designed by Maya Lin, was dedicated by the Southern Poverty Law Center in 1989. Dexter Avenue King Memorial Baptist Church, where Martin Luther King Jr. was pastor from 1954 to 1960, sits steps from the Alabama State Capitol.

A visitor's day here is planned around those places, and the small businesses nearby get found in the gaps. A typical search pattern looks like this:

  1. The night beforeWhich sites are open tomorrow, and how far apart are they? A guide or shuttle with a clear page about routes and times gets booked here.
  2. Mid-morningCoffee within walking distance, with a menu that loads on a phone. A PDF menu loses this customer.
  3. LunchCan a group of eight sit together, and is there parking? Restaurants that answer on the page get the call.
  4. AfternoonA bookstore, a gift shop or a second museum. Hours matter more than photos.
  5. EveningDinner and a place to stay. Small inns and guesthouses that explain the walk to downtown win over a faceless listing.

None of this needs a large site. It needs the facts a stranger would ask for, written for someone standing on the sidewalk.

The capital, the base and the plant

The visitor trade is only part of the picture. As the state capital, Montgomery is home to the Capitol and state agencies. Maxwell-Gunter Air Force Base is headquarters to Air University, the Air Force's center for professional military education, with the 42nd Air Base Wing as host. Its Reserve flying unit, renamed the 908th Flying Training Wing in July 2024, now trains crews on the MH-139A Grey Wolf helicopter. Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama began production at its Montgomery plant in May 2005, and the AP reported nearly 3,000 full-time employees there in 2018.
