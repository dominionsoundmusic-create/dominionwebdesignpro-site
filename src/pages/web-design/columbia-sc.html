Web design Columbia SC businesses need around Fort Jackson

Fort Jackson is the Army's largest initial-entry training center. The Army says it trains about half of the soldiers who enter the service each year, roughly 35,000 in basic combat training and another 8,000 in advanced individual training. Basic training lasts ten weeks, and graduations are held nearly every week: the Army describes a weekly basic training graduation, and Columbia's visitors bureau has described families arriving for Thursday morning ceremonies.

Every class that finishes means relatives traveling to see a graduation. Many arrive from other states, stay a night or two and look for somewhere to eat, sleep, get a haircut or fix a flat. They do not know Columbia. They search, compare the first few results and pick one.

Those visitors want plain answers: are you open on the days they are in town, can they make a reservation for a group of eight, how far are you from the post, is there parking for a big vehicle. A site that answers that in the first screen is doing its job.

What a visiting family checks before choosing

  • Hours for the exact days they are here, including any special graduation-week hours.
  • A tap-to-call button and an address that opens in their map app.
  • Whether you take groups, reservations or walk-ins.
  • Rough prices, or at least a menu or a service list they can scan.
  • Photos of the real place, so they know they have arrived.

None of that needs a large site. The Starter package covers it in five pages, with Google Maps and hours on the page.
