They drive in from small towns in Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma and Louisiana for a doctor's appointment, a day of shopping or a part they cannot get at home, and most of them check a business online before they get in the car. Dominion Web Design Pro is Maurice Johnson, a Texan building sites by hand from Big Sandy. You see a free demo first and pay one printed price.

Texarkana is two cities sharing one name, with State Line Avenue running down the border and a post office and federal courthouse that sits in both states at once. This page is about the Texas side, a city of about 36,000 people (35,971 in the Census Bureau's 2024 estimate).

A 2025 overview from TexAmericas Center calls Texarkana the medical hub of a large rural healthcare market. CHRISTUS St. Michael has long been the biggest name, and in November 2024 CHRISTUS Health finished buying Wadley Regional Medical Center out of the Steward Health Care bankruptcy, keeping nearly all of its clinicians and staff.

Hospitals pull in a whole ring of smaller businesses: specialty clinics, physical therapists, home health agencies, pharmacies, medical supply stores, and the restaurants and hotels where families wait. A patient from two counties away searching for a clinic needs the street address, the right entrance, parking and hours on the first screen. If the site buries those, the phone rings with questions instead of appointments.

Three Texarkana businesses and what each page has to carry

The clinic down the street from the hospital

Insurance accepted, a map to the door, what to bring to a first visit, and a phone line answered when the front desk is busy.

The shop near Richmond Road

Richmond Road near Interstate 30 is the main shopping strip, with newer growth to the west toward the University Avenue interchange. A small shop there needs hours and a reason to stop that a big box cannot give.

The trade that covers the whole area

A plumber, roofer or electrician working Bowie County and beyond should list the towns served, the licenses held and an easy way to ask for a quote.

Paychecks from the depot and a growing campus

Red River Army Depot, in Bowie County, began in World War II when more than 20,000 construction workers turned 18,000 acres into an ordnance depot, and it still ranks among the area's major employers, along with CHRISTUS St. Michael, Cooper Tire and Texas A&M University-Texarkana. Depot workers and contractors are local customers for every kind of service.
