Writing for a family that just got orders to Dyess

A household moving to Dyess may be choosing a plumber, a pediatric dentist or a dog groomer from another state, before the moving truck is even packed. They cannot ask a neighbor yet. Your website is the neighbor.

That means a few things in practice. Say which parts of Abilene and which nearby towns you serve. Put hours and a phone number that taps to call at the top of the page. If you offer anything specific to military families, describe it exactly, without vague promises. And keep the site current, because a newcomer who calls a disconnected number will not try twice.

The Storybook Capital and why a small story matters online

In 2015 the Texas Legislature named Abilene the Storybook Capital of Texas, pointing to the National Center for Children's Illustrated Literature and the storybook sculptures placed around downtown. In 2018 the city's Cultural Affairs Council and Convention and Visitors Bureau went further and trademarked the name Storybook Capital of America. A new sculpture is unveiled each year during the Children's Art and Literacy Festival in June.

There is a lesson in that for a small business. Abilene did not wait for someone to hand it a title; it told its own story in specific terms and stuck with it. A good small business site does the same: who you are, what you do, which part of town you are in, written by someone who asked instead of copying a template.
