Web design in San Angelo works best when it starts with who walks through your door. In this town that is often someone new: an airman or soldier who just arrived at Goodfellow for training, a freshman at Angelo State, or a ranch family in from a county where the nearest store is an hour away. None of them know your shop yet. They know how to search on a phone, and they decide quickly.

Dominion Web Design Pro is Maurice Johnson, a fellow Texan building sites by hand from Big Sandy. You see a free demo before anything is paid.

Who a San Angelo website is really talking to

Goodfellow Air Force Base. The 17th Training Wing there trains intelligence and cryptologic specialists for the Air Force, Army, Navy and Marine Corps, and runs fire protection training for firefighters from every branch. Students arrive, train and move on, so there is always a fresh group looking for a barber, a gym, a mechanic or a place to eat.

Angelo State University. Students and visiting parents search for the same basics, often from out of town, and usually at the start of a semester.

Ranch country. San Angelo grew up as a market for sheep, goats, wool and mohair. A Texas historical marker calls it the major market center for those animals and their fiber, and Texas A&M still runs its wool and mohair research lab here. Ranchers come to town for vets, feed, equipment, repairs and supplies.

Small fixes that matter most here

  • Say where you are in plain wordsNewcomers do not know local landmarks yet. Give the street, the cross street and a sentence of directions.
  • Put prices or price ranges on the pageTrainees and students on fixed budgets compare before they call. A range beats silence.
  • Make hours easy to updateRanch supply stores and repair shops change hours by season; the site should change the same day.
  • List the towns you coverIf you deliver or do service calls outside the city, name the towns, and say where you stop.

Web design San Angelo shops downtown can use

Downtown sits along the Concho River, where the River Walk has gardens, parks and a River Stage that hosts concerts. Historic Concho Avenue is known for one-of-a-kind shops, and the Chicken Farm Art Center, a former chicken farm, now houses artist studios working in pottery, metal, glass and more. Visitors who come for a show or an art weekend look up restaurants and stores nearby on their phones.

Google ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence, so a downtown boutique should say it is downtown, and a contractor should name the parts of town and the nearby towns it works in.

Local help for Concho Valley owners

The ASU Small Business Development Center has served the ten-county Concho Valley since 1990 with no-cost one-on-one advising and training, and its rural advisors travel out to smaller communities. The City of San Angelo Development Corporation (COSADC) is the city's Type B economic development corporation, and it contracts business recruitment and marketing to the San Angelo Chamber of Commerce.
