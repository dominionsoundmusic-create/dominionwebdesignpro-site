The boating economy behind Fort Lauderdale website design

The city's official seal carries the nickname "Venice of America," and the waterways earn it: about 165 miles of them run inside city limits, part of roughly 300 miles of inland waterways across Greater Fort Lauderdale. The Intracoastal Waterway, the New River and a web of residential canals mean many homes come with a dock, and many docks come with a boat that needs work.

  • About 142,000 jobsMarine industry jobs across South Florida, according to economic impact research by the Marine Industries Association of South Florida, as cited by the Greater Fort Lauderdale Alliance. Broward County holds a large share of them.
  • About 184,000 residentsThe city's population in the Census Bureau's July 2023 estimate, up from 182,760 in the 2020 count.

Brokers and yacht sales firms like to call the city the yachting capital of the world. Whatever you think of the title, the trade behind it is made of small operators: mobile mechanics, detailers, canvas and upholstery shops, varnish crews, delivery captains, brokers and the yards that haul boats out. Most of them get work by referral, and most of them are checked online before the phone rings.

What each marine trade's pages should say

A boat owner hiring help wants the same three answers from every trade: can you do this job, on a boat like mine, where it sits. Here is how that plays out by trade.

TradeWhat the owner typesWhat the page must show

Notice that none of these needs a fancy site. They need the right words on the page, in the order a boat owner reads them.

Boat show week, walked through

The Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show is the city's biggest yearly test for these businesses. The 2025 show ran October 29 through November 2 across seven sites: the Broward County Convention Center, Bahia Mar Yachting Center, Hall of Fame Marina, Las Olas Marina, the 17th Street Yacht Basin, Pier Sixty-Six North and a superyacht area at Pier Sixty-Six South. WLRN reported the show's backers put its economic impact at nearly $1.8 billion.

Take a typical case, a made-up one to show the idea: a two-person mobile detailing crew. In the weeks before the show, exhibitors and private owners want boats turned out spotless. In the days after, visitors who met the crew on a dock look them up by name. The site needs a short notice at the top about show-week availability, a quote form that asks for the slip location and boat length, and a page for each kind of detail they do. When the show ends, the notice comes down and the regular pages carry on.

That change takes a few minutes if someone is looking after the site. On our hosting plan, that someone is us, at no extra charge per edit.

Broward help for small businesses

  • Florida SBDC at FAUFlorida Atlantic University hosts the Small Business Development Center office serving Broward and Palm Beach counties, and its contact page now lists the Broward mailing address on FAU's Davie campus, with appointments required. In 2021 it announced two branch offices in Davie, one at FAU's Davie West building and one at the Town of Davie's Community Services offices. It is part of the statewide Florida SBDC Network, whose lead host is the University of West Florida.
  • Broward County OESBDThe county's Office of Economic and Small Business Development certifies local firms that want county contracts. Its Small Business Enterprise program is for firms with 15 or fewer permanent full-time employees; applicants need a Broward business tax receipt, a location in the county and at least a year in operation.
  • Federal certificationsThe same office handles Disadvantaged Business Enterprise and Airport Concessions certification through the Florida Unified Certification Program.

If you are bidding on public work, the SBE or CBE status belongs on your site too, stated exactly as the certificate reads.
