The Golden Triangle, and the well that started it

On January 10, 1901, the Lucas gusher at Spindletop, just south of Beaumont, blew oil far above the derrick and took nine days to bring under control. Beaumont went from about 9,000 people to about 30,000 within a few months, and companies that grew into Gulf Oil and Texaco trace their start to that field.

More than a century later, Beaumont, Port Arthur and Orange are still called the Golden Triangle, and refining still shapes the local economy. ExxonMobil's Beaumont refinery finished a $2 billion expansion in 2023 that added 250,000 barrels a day, bringing it past 630,000 barrels a day and making it one of the largest refineries in the country.
