Web design in West Palm Beach for owners who live here part of the year

{{ img("demo-images/real-estate/1.jpg", "Stucco house with red shutters, a red front door and trimmed hedges at sunset on a quiet residential street", 1344, 768) }}

Winter brings the second population. WPTV, citing county figures, reported that about 145,000 seasonal residents come to Palm Beach County each year, raising the population by roughly 11 percent. The county's own planning profile counts seasonal residents separately from year-round ones, working from the number of seasonal homes in the Census and the size of a typical household.

Every one of those seasonal homes sits empty for months. Someone checks it after storms, runs the water, keeps the pool clean, fixes the screen door and has the place ready when the owners fly back. Those businesses sell to customers who mostly meet them online and by phone.

That changes what the website has to do. A homeowner in Ohio cannot drive past your shop or see your truck in the neighborhood. Your pages stand in for the handshake, so they need to answer the questions an absent owner worries about most: who will be in my house, how will I know it was checked, and what happens if something goes wrong while I am away.

What an absent owner looks for before hiring you

Who walks in the door

Your name, a real photo of you or your crew at work, and the insurance or license you actually hold, stated as it reads on the paper. Leave out anything you cannot show.

How they hear back

What a visit report looks like, how often you go, and whether updates come by text, email or photos. A sample report with the names removed answers this better than a paragraph.

What happens in a storm

What you do before and after severe weather, how fast you can get there, and how they reach you when the phone lines are busy. Keep it to plain promises you can keep.

Pool services, cleaners, handymen, landscapers and concierge firms all fit the same pattern. Write those three answers clearly and the site does its job while you are out on a route.

Wall Street South and the work it brought downtown

Since the pandemic, finance firms have opened offices or moved headquarters to the Palm Beaches in large numbers, which is how the area picked up the nickname Wall Street South. The county's Business Development Board counts at least 250 financial firms that have moved in, a figure The Spectator reported along with names such as BlackRock, Citadel, Goldman Sachs and Elliott Investment Management. Treat that count as the board's own estimate. Two concrete cases: in 2026 Wells Fargo announced it would move its wealth management headquarters to One Flagler in West Palm Beach, and the Maryland mortgage lender NewDay USA opened a second headquarters downtown.

Big firms are not who we build for. The opportunity is around them. New households need accountants who handle a move from a high-tax state, real estate attorneys, interior designers, dog walkers, car services, tutors and restaurants that can take a client dinner on short notice. Those owners compete for people who have never heard of them and will judge them in seconds on a phone.

For a restaurant, that means a menu that loads as real text, a private dining page that says how many guests the room seats, and a way to book that works on the first tap. For a professional firm, it means a page for each service you actually offer and a short, honest bio of who will do the work.

Newcomers from New York and Connecticut also bring expectations. They are used to firms that answer email the same day and list prices or ranges where they can. Saying how you work, in writing, earns trust before the first call.

West Palm Beach website design that keeps up with changing names

The city had 124,130 residents in the Census Bureau's July 2023 estimate, up from 117,322 at the 2020 count. Its landmarks have a habit of changing names, and that matters on a website, because customers search with whichever name they know.

  • CityPlace, the downtown shopping and entertainment center, was renamed Rosemary Square in 2019 by its owner, Related Companies, as part of a $550 million plan. In 2024 it went back to CityPlace. A business nearby may need both names on its page for a while, because people learned both.
  • Lake Worth became Lake Worth Beach after a March 2019 referendum that passed by 62 votes. Service businesses that cover it should use the current name and expect searches under the old one.
  • Clematis Street downtown is best known for its restaurants and nightlife, so a spot there lives or dies on hours that are right and a menu people can read on a phone.
  • Northwood Village, a small district about a mile and a half north of downtown along Northwood Road, is full of locally owned restaurants, boutiques and galleries.
  • Palm Beach, across the lagoon, is its own town. Searchers type "palm beach web design" when they mean either place, so a business should say exactly which side of the bridge it is on and which side it serves.

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is the part your own words control. A page that names the neighborhoods and towns you truly serve, under the names people use today, gives Google something to match. Pro includes ten city pages, each one researched and written about that place, which suits a service business covering much of the county. Nobody can promise you a spot in the results, and we will not.

Design, development and what you actually need

People who search "west palm beach web development" are often asking whether they need a programmer. Most local firms do not. A photographer who shoots listings for agents, for example, needs a fast gallery, a page for each kind of shoot, a booking request form and a clear service area. That is design and writing, which is what we do.

Development means software: a portal where clients log in to see their own records, an online store with inventory and shipping, or a booking engine tied to your own systems. Those are not in our packages. If that is what you need, a development firm is the better hire, and we would rather tell you on day one than after a deposit.

Free and local help for Palm Beach County owners

  • Florida SBDC at FAUFlorida Atlantic University hosts the Small Business Development Center office for Broward and Palm Beach counties. Its consultants offer confidential, one-on-one advice at no cost, in person or online, plus low-cost training.
  • City Office of Small Business ProgramsThe City of West Palm Beach offers training for businesses inside city limits and helps them earn the city's small business certification so they can bid on city contracts. In 2023 its First Steps grants gave a total of $500,000 to 43 small, minority- and women-owned businesses, chosen from more than 230 applicants.
  • Chamber of Commerce of the Palm BeachesA business association based in West Palm Beach whose leaders list workshops and training for owners, and advocacy with local and state officials, among its main jobs.

If you hold a city small business certification, put it on your site exactly as the certificate reads. Anyone checking your eligibility for city work will look for it there.
