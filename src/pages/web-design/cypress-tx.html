Cypress is an unincorporated community in Harris County, roughly 24 miles northwest of downtown Houston along US 290. It has no mayor, no city limits and no city economic development office. What it does have is a set of names everyone uses instead, and your website should speak them fluently.

Web design in Cypress, where a ZIP code does the work of city limits

Large-scale homebuilding that began in the 1980s turned a once rural area into one of the biggest suburban areas around Houston, and the whole community sits inside Houston's extraterritorial jurisdiction without being part of the city. Ask a resident where they live and you will hear one of four kinds of answer:

  • A community. Bridgeland, the Howard Hughes master-planned community; Towne Lake, built around a lake of about 300 acres; Fairfield, developed beginning in 1988 off US 290; Coles Crossing.
  • A ZIP code. Real estate listings for those four often group them under 77433, and buyers and customers use the number as shorthand.
  • A school district. Cy-Fair ISD calls itself the third-largest district in Texas, with close to 118,000 students, and families often describe home by their school.
  • A highway. US 290 and the Grand Parkway frame much of the growth, and Barker Cypress Road is where Lone Star College CyFair has its campus.

A Cypress website that uses those names reads like it was written by someone who knows the area. One that only says "Houston area" reads like everyone else's.

A suburb that is filling in, not spreading out

For years the Cypress story was raw growth: new sections, new schools, new rooftops every season. The numbers now point to a more settled phase. Cy-Fair ISD reported enrollment down 2.7 percent in October 2025 compared with the year before, and a demographic report prepared for the district expects a slight dip, from about 118,057 students to 116,757, by 2034, partly because there is less open land left for new homes.

That matters for a small business. When the flood of brand new households slows, the customers you compete for are the ones already here, who have already picked a dentist, a lawn crew or a pediatrician once. Winning them over takes a site that gives a reason to switch: clearer answers, easier booking, and proof you serve their exact corner of Cypress.

One Cypress practice, two ways to describe it

Version two says she treats patients from Towne Lake, Bridgeland and Coles Crossing, lists her hours around the school day for parents, explains what a first visit involves, and gives a map with the nearest Highway 290 exit.
