Downtown McKinney: a square that still does what it was planned to do

McKinney was founded in 1848 as the seat of Collin County, about 30 miles north of Dallas, and the courthouse square sat at the center of the original town plan. Like many Texas downtowns it slumped when new highways drew traffic away, then came back. McKinney has been part of the Texas Historical Commission's Texas Main Street Program since 2003, a program built on the Main Street approach that grew out of the National Trust for Historic Preservation, and the shops on the square are mostly locally owned.

A few blocks away, Chestnut Square Historic Village is home to the McKinney Farmers Market and offers tours that include the 1854 Faires House, the oldest home in McKinney. Those draws bring people downtown who have never been before. They search on a phone for lunch, a gift shop or a place that is open past five, and they choose from what loads.

What a shop on the square needs online

  • Current hours, including special event hours, on the home page.
  • What you actually sell, with photos, so a visitor knows whether to walk over.
  • Where to park and how to find you from the courthouse.
  • A tap-to-call number and a map at the top, not buried in the footer.

The other McKinney: master-planned neighborhoods and the trades that serve them

Drive west from the square and the city changes character. Stonebridge Ranch, Craig Ranch and the newer subdivisions north toward US 380 are full of families who need the same things every week: lawn care, pest control, pool and patio work, house cleaning, tutoring, pediatric care, auto repair. Many of those businesses are run by one or two people out of a truck or a home office, and most of their new customers arrive by search, not by a sign.

For a service company here, the website has a narrower job than a shop's. It needs to say exactly which services you do, which neighborhoods and nearby towns you drive to, and how fast someone can reach you. A homeowner in Craig Ranch comparing three fence companies on a phone will call the one whose page answers "do you come out here, and what do you do" without scrolling. Photos of real jobs in real North Texas yards help more than any slogan.

The same homeowner checks reviews. On the Pro package your Google reviews show on the site itself, so a satisfied customer in Stonebridge does some of the selling for the next one.

An airport terminal on the way

McKinney National Airport has served private aircraft for years. In July 2025 the city broke ground on a passenger terminal of about 46,000 square feet with four gates, expandable to six, and a 980-space parking lot. Avelo Airlines signed on as the first commercial carrier in December 2025, and in September 2026 the city said the terminal was on schedule to open on November 11, 2026. Avelo has announced nonstop flights to Las Vegas and four Florida cities, with Atlanta, Denver, Nashville and New Orleans planned from mid-December.

Once scheduled flights begin, they bring visitors who land with no idea what is in town and a phone in their hand. That rewards the businesses that are easy to find: hotels and restaurants first, then car services, salons, florists and anyone a traveler might need on short notice. It also brings new businesses competing for the same searches. Either way, being findable in McKinney matters more each year, not less.
