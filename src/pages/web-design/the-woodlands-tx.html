A township of villages, and an address that confuses outsiders

The Woodlands had 114,436 residents in the 2020 census, which is bigger than many Texas cities, yet it is not a city. It is run by The Woodlands Township, a special purpose district created after the Texas Legislature passed enabling legislation in 2007, with a seven-member board elected by voters in both Montgomery and Harris counties. Residents voted on becoming a city in November 2021, and about two thirds said no.

Inside the township, people identify by village. The Township lists village associations including Alden Bridge, Cochran's Crossing, College Park, Creekside Park, Grogan's Mill, Indian Springs, Panther Creek and Sterling Ridge, and its website offers an address lookup to find which village an address belongs to. Creekside Park is the only village that reaches into Harris County.

For someone in Ohio or California, none of that is obvious. A web design The Woodlands TX newcomers can follow says it plainly: which villages you serve, what the mailing address really is, and how far you will drive.

Why so many of your customers are still packing boxes

Big employers keep pulling households into the area. ExxonMobil announced in 2022 that it would move its corporate headquarters from Irving to its campus in Spring, a campus built for up to 10,000 employees and visitors. Hewlett Packard Enterprise opened its headquarters in the CityPlace development in Spring in 2022, with room for more than 2,200 Houston-area employees.

Households relocating for jobs like these research before they arrive. That favors the business whose site answers questions without a phone call: services in plain words, prices or a price range where you can give one, photos of the real place, and an easy way to book.

What a newcomer-ready site in The Woodlands should show

  • Your village or the villages you cover, written out, not just "Houston area".
  • Whether you are near Town Center, where the Waterway connects Market Street, Waterway Square, Hughes Landing and The Woodlands Mall, or out in the neighborhoods.
  • Hours, parking and whether walk-ins are welcome, for people who have never been inside.
  • A short "new to the area?" note if you take new patients, students or members.
  • A contact form that emails you, and a phone number that rings.
