Many arrived in the last few years for jobs east of town. Both groups pick a plumber, a dentist or a lunch spot the same way: on a phone, in a few seconds.

The industrial park that changed who lives here

East of Reno, in Storey County, the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center covers more than 100,000 acres. The park already had many tenants when Tesla chose it for its Gigafactory, announced in 2014, which became its best-known tenant. Switch began building a data center campus there in 2015, and in 2017 a Google subsidiary bought 1,210 acres in the park.

The region's job recruiter, the Economic Development Authority of Western Nevada (EDAWN), is a private nonprofit founded in 1983 that focuses on companies that export most of what they make. Those employers bring workers, and the workers need everything else: haircuts, car repair, house cleaning, child care, dinner. The Census Bureau counted 264,165 people in Reno in 2020 and estimated 274,915 by July 2023, a rise of about 4%, and a HUD market profile put the Reno metro area at about 505,600 people at the start of 2024.

A family that just moved from another state does not know which shops are good. They search, they read your site, and they decide. That is where a clear website pays for itself.

Midtown and the Riverwalk, the local side of Reno

Away from the casinos, Midtown runs along South Virginia Street south of downtown, with murals, cafes, thrift stores, patio pubs and locally owned shops. Downtown along the Truckee River, the Riverwalk Merchants Association brings together dozens of nearby restaurants, shops, salons and museums, and it hosts a wine walk on the third Saturday of every month.

For these shops a website does three jobs. It confirms you are still open, it tells a visitor which block you are on, and it lists the events that bring people downtown. A wine walk stop that posts its hours and a photo of the storefront is easy to find the next week.

For a Midtown restaurant

Put the menu on the page as text, not a PDF, with today's hours and a tap-to-call button. Add a short note on patio season and private events. That is what a hungry visitor looks for first.

For a photographer or studio

A gallery of real work, the kinds of sessions you shoot, a starting price if you are comfortable sharing one, and a booking form. Lake Tahoe trips bring couples and families who want photos, so say whether you travel there.

Web design for Reno businesses that serve the whole valley

Residents name their part of town when they search. Sparks is its own city, and neighborhoods such as the South Meadows, Northwest Reno, Somersett and Damonte Ranch each have their own feel. Google says local results weigh relevance, distance and prominence. A landscaper or HVAC company whose site names the areas it covers gives Google something to match; one that just says "Northern Nevada" does not.
