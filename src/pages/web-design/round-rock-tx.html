Dell's hometown, and a fast-growing city

Dell opened its global headquarters in Round Rock on August 2, 1994, under a 1993 economic development agreement with the city that the council extended in 2024 to run through 2099, and the company's address is still One Dell Way. The 2020 census counted 119,468 residents, and the city has kept growing since.

Workdays bring their own traffic. Along University Boulevard, Baylor Scott & White Medical Center Round Rock sits at 300 University Boulevard, and Texas State University and Austin Community College both run Round Rock campuses with health care programs; the Texas A&M School of Medicine has a campus in town too. Staff and students there need lunch, child care, a dentist and an oil change near work.

Web design in Round Rock, TX for the Sports Capital of Texas

The city started calling itself the Sports Capital of Texas in a 2004 campaign and later registered the phrase as a federal trademark. It is a brand, not a state title, but the fields behind it are real. Old Settlers Park covers about 645 acres next to Dell Diamond, and its Multipurpose Complex, a $27 million, 60-acre addition, opened in 2017. Dell Diamond is home to the Round Rock Express, the Texas Rangers' Triple-A team.
