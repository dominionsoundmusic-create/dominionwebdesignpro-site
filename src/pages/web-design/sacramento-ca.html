Website design in Sacramento when the state is the biggest customer in town

The City of Sacramento says it has a higher concentration of government jobs per capita than anywhere else in California. A 2019 regional workforce study, using 2017 data, counted almost 90,000 state government jobs in Sacramento County alone, part of roughly a quarter of all state government jobs in California held in the six-county region.

That shapes the private economy around it. Engineering and IT consultants, printers, caterers, janitorial and facility firms, trainers and translators all sell to agencies. Then there are the businesses that serve state workers directly: lunch spots near the Capitol, dry cleaners, gyms, child care, tax preparers. Each group needs a site that speaks to its own buyer.

California's Small Business certification, explained plainly

The state's Department of General Services runs a Small Business certification through its Office of Small Business and DVBE Services. To qualify, a business must be independently owned and operated, not dominant in its field, have its principal office in California with its owners or officers living in the state, and, together with affiliates, have 100 or fewer employees and average annual gross receipts of $19 million or less over the previous three tax years. A small business holding the certification with gross receipts of $6 million or less is automatically designated a microbusiness. DGS adjusts those dollar limits every two years.

Why firms bother: under DGS policy each state department sets an annual goal of at least 25% small business participation in its contracting, and at least 3% for disabled veteran business enterprises. Small businesses holding the certification can receive a 5% bid preference against firms that are not. Applications and the public search of firms that hold it both live in Cal eProcure, which is where a state buyer checks a vendor's status.

  • If you hold a certification, say which one on your About or Capabilities page and keep it current.
  • Describe your services in the plain words a contract analyst would search for, not in internal jargon.
  • Give buyers a capabilities page they can forward: services, service area, past project types, contact.
  • Never claim a certification you do not hold. Buyers check.

From Midtown to Elk Grove: the places customers name

Residents search by neighborhood. Midtown sits just east of downtown; East Sacramento lies beyond it with McKinley Park; Land Park centers on William Land Park south of the central city; and Oak Park sits between Midtown and Land Park. The R Street corridor, a few blocks south of the Capitol, grew up as a warehouse district along the Sacramento Valley Railroad, which began running to Folsom in 1856, and its old buildings now hold restaurants and galleries. Up north, the City of Sacramento has offered grants of up to $240,000 for storefront improvements to North Sacramento businesses along Del Paso Boulevard.

Beyond the city, which the California Department of Finance put at about 520,000 people in January 2024, the suburbs are large towns of their own: Elk Grove at about 179,000, Folsom at about 88,000 and Rancho Cordova at about 82,000, with Roseville across the line in Placer County. A landscaper in Elk Grove or a contractor working Folsom and Rancho Cordova wins work town by town. Google says local results weigh relevance, distance and prominence; a page that plainly says what you do in Folsom is relevant to a Folsom search in a way a single "serving the Sacramento area" line is not.
