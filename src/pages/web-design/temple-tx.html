Web design in Temple, TX starts with knowing who is searching. This is the Temple in Bell County, Texas, not the university in Philadelphia that shows up in the same searches. It is a medical town on I-35, and a lot of the people looking for a sitter, a lunch spot, a physical therapist or an apartment work long shifts at a hospital and have five minutes to decide. Maurice Johnson, a Texan in Big Sandy, writes those sites by hand and shows you a free demo before you pay anything.

Two hospitals, a warehouse row and a growing town

Baylor Scott & White, which the Temple Economic Development Corporation calls the largest not-for-profit health care system in Texas, has its medical center here. So does the Olin E. Teague Veterans' Medical Center, the main campus of the Central Texas Veterans Health Care System, which serves veterans across 39 Texas counties. Distribution is the other big employer: McLane Company is based in Temple, and H-E-B and Walmart run distribution centers here.

The town is growing to match. The Census Bureau put Temple at 82,073 people in 2020 and 93,095 by its July 2023 estimate, a rise of 13.4% in three years. New residents mean people choosing a dentist, a mechanic and a hair stylist for the first time, usually on a phone.

Hospital staff

Shift workers need hours that are on the page, early or late openings stated plainly, and booking that does not require a call during a twelve-hour shift.

Veterans and their families

Many come to Temple for care. They look for services near the VA campus, clear directions and someone who will explain things patiently.

Newcomers on I-35

Families moving in for new jobs search for everything at once. A site that names the neighborhoods you serve wins the first call.

Temple web design for small practices and home health

Big hospitals draw a ring of small health businesses: physical and speech therapy, counseling, home health agencies, medical equipment, chiropractors and dentists. Their websites have the same job. Say what you treat, who you see, where you are, what plans you take, and how to make the first appointment.

  • Services in plain words a patient uses, not billing codes
  • Accepted insurance listed only as you confirm it
  • A referral page for doctors' offices and case managers
  • Hours, parking and accessibility, stated up front

Where Temple owners can get free help

The Temple EDC, formed in 1989, works with a network that includes the Temple Chamber of Commerce, Bell County, Temple College and the Small Business Development Center.
