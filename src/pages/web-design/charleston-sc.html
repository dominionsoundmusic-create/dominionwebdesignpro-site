Charleston website design for work a review board will see

{{ img("demo-images/roofing/3.jpg", "Roofer in a white hard hat and yellow shirt kneeling on a steep clay tile roof under a pale blue sky", 1344, 768) }}

The City of Charleston's Board of Architectural Review looks at new construction and exterior alterations in the city's historic districts that can be seen from a public street. In the Old and Historic District, demolitions go to the Board no matter how old the building is. Smaller items such as painting, signs, sitework and repairs are usually approved at staff level, and a staff decision can be appealed to the Board. The Board itself is split in two: one panel for large projects and one for small ones.

For a roofer, a window restorer, a mason or a painter, that process is part of the sale. A homeowner who has heard stories about approvals wants to know whether you will handle the paperwork, how long the review tends to add, and what happens if the first submittal is sent back.

So the site should say it. Not with promises about approvals, which nobody can make, but with a calm explanation of how you work: who prepares the drawings, when you submit, what you need from the owner, and how you keep the job moving while you wait. That one page does more for a peninsula contractor than any slogan.

Charleston SC web design by part of town

The city had about 155,400 residents in the Census Bureau's 2023 estimate, and Charleston County about 436,200 in 2025. Customers rarely search for "Charleston" alone; they search for where they are.

King Street and Upper King

King Street was named one of the American Planning Association's Great Places in America in 2014. Its upper end is often called the Design and Dining District, packed with restaurants, bars and independent shops.

Mount Pleasant

Its own town, on the east side of the Cooper River, linked to the peninsula by the Ravenel Bridge. A Mount Pleasant customer looking for a dentist or a fence builder expects to see Mount Pleasant named on your site if you really serve it.

West Ashley

The part of the city west of the Ashley River. Residents there search with “West Ashley” in the query, so a salon or repair shop there should use the name the way locals do.

James Island and Johns Island

Islands reached by bridges: the Wappoo Bridge links West Ashley to James Island, and Johns Island lies across the Stono River beyond it. A lawn crew or a pool service should say plainly whether it crosses the bridges.

North Charleston

Its own city with its own addresses, and the home of the Charleston Area SBDC and the local SCORE chapter office. A business here should say North Charleston, not just Charleston.

Summerville

About 51,900 people lived inside town limits in the 2023 estimate. Owners there search for web design in Summerville, SC, and their customers search by town name too.
