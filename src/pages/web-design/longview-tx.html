A working town with money moving through it

Longview's paychecks come from places that make and move things. Eastman runs a large chemical manufacturing site here. In 2021 Gap Inc. announced a distribution center of roughly 850,000 square feet in Longview, a $140 million project built first to ship Old Navy's online orders. Eastman also planned a molecular recycling plant here; in 2025 the federal government cancelled its $375 million grant for that project, and that August Eastman said it was delaying construction and reassessing the project's scope and timing. Whatever happens with that plant, the pattern is clear: big employers bring in workers, and workers need everything a small business sells.

That is where the opportunity sits for a local shop. New arrivals do not have a barber yet, or a family doctor, or a guy who fixes garage doors. They search for one. The business that comes up with a clear, honest, fast site gets the first call. The one with only a Facebook page usually does not.

If that sounds like you, the right site is not a big one. It is one that says exactly what you do, where you do it, and how to reach you, on a phone, in a few seconds. Starter covers that for most single-location businesses. If you send crews to five or six towns, Pro adds a city page for each of them.

What we usually find on Longview business websites

Most small business sites in Gregg County fall into one of three situations. Each one has a plain fix.

Only a Facebook page

Google has almost nothing to show when someone searches your name, never mind your service. The fix is a real site on a domain you own, with a page for each thing you do.

A site nobody has touched

The phone number on it does not match the one on the truck, and the hours changed two summers ago. The fix is a rebuild, and after that, updates are part of hosting and maintenance.

A rented platform

Stop paying and the site disappears, and sometimes the domain was never in your name. The fix is a one-time build that you own outright, with the domain registered to you.

How web design in Longview, TX turns into phone calls

Longview has fewer competitors than Dallas, which helps, and fewer searches, which means each one matters more. Three things do most of the work, and every one of them is in the build rather than sold as an extra.

  1. A complete map listingFor anything typed with "near me", the map results sit above the regular results. A filled-in Google Business Profile with real photos and reviews does more for a Longview shop than almost anything else. Pro and Agency include setting it up.
  2. A page for each service and each townOne page that says "serving East Texas" does not tell Google much. Separate pages for what you do in Longview, Kilgore, White Oak or Hallsville give it something to match, as long as each one is written for that place.
  3. Speed on a phoneMost people looking you up are standing in a parking lot or a driveway. If the page takes five seconds, they are gone. Every site we build is made to load fast on a phone.
