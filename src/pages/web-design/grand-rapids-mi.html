Furniture City still makes things

The nickname is earned. William Haldane opened one of the city's first cabinet shops in 1836, and at the 1876 Centennial Exhibition in Philadelphia several Grand Rapids companies won awards for their furniture. The Furniture Manufacturers Association formed in Grand Rapids in 1881. The Depression and World War II ended the peak years for household furniture, and the region turned toward office furniture instead.

Steelcase, founded in 1912 as the Metal Office Furniture Company, is headquartered in Grand Rapids. Herman Miller grew up in nearby Zeeland and Haworth in Holland, and together the three became giants of office design. Large manufacturers buy parts, fabric, finishing and freight from smaller firms, and for a small supplier the website is often the first thing a new buyer checks.

The Medical Mile on Michigan Street

The city's second big cluster is health care. Its anchors arrived over more than a century, and hospital, research and medical school buildings now line the corridor.

  • 1875St. Mark's Home and Hospital opens, founded by parishioners of St. Mark's Episcopal Church. It is now Corewell Health Butterworth Hospital.
  • 1996 to 2000Jay and Betty Van Andel found the Van Andel Institute, a research institute focused on cancer and other diseases, which opens its doors on Michigan Street in 2000.
  • 2010Michigan State University's College of Human Medicine opens the Secchia Center as its headquarters, with backing from local hospital systems and the institute.

Around a cluster like that grow the small businesses that serve its people: physical therapists, counselors, home care agencies, medical billing offices, caterers, cleaners and the lunch spots nearby. Their sites need clear service lists, parking and access details, and booking steps a tired person can follow on a phone.

Website design Grand Rapids neighborhoods search for

Grand Rapids has organized several of its commercial streets into corridor improvement districts, under a Michigan law first passed in 2005 to keep business districts from declining. Uptown was the first. Its Corridor Improvement Authority helps fund projects in the East Fulton, Wealthy Street, East Hills and Eastown business districts, while a separate business improvement district handles upkeep. Across the river, a West Side district takes in Bridge Street, Stockbridge, West Fulton and Seward.

People use those names when they search. "Coffee Eastown" and "barber Bridge Street" are real ways to look for a business, and a shop that names its district in the page title and first paragraph gives Google a clear match. Google says local results turn on relevance, distance and prominence, and a district name helps with the first two. A trade working the suburbs (Wyoming, Kentwood, Grandville, Rockford) needs the same treatment: a page for each town it wants work in.
