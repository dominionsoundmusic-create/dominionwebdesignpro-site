What our web design services cover

Dominion Web Design Pro offers web design services for local businesses: the plumber who needs the phone to ring, the salon owner who is tired of a page nobody updates, the small law office that wants its practice areas explained properly. Every service on this page ends in the same thing, a working website where each page was written by hand for that business and the towns it serves.

What changes from one service to the next is the starting point and the reader. A brand new business needs different pages than a twenty-year shop with an outdated site. A roofer's customer is reading on a phone after a storm; a therapist's client is reading at night, deciding whether to reach out at all. The services below are grouped so you can find the one that sounds like you.

One thing does not change: you see a demo of your site before there is any talk of money.

Three ways to think about which one you need

Most owners fit one of these situations. Find yours, and the service follows from it.

You have no website, or one you are embarrassed by

Start with small business website design. It covers the pages every local business needs and how they are written. If budget is the worry, affordable website design is honest about what a low one-time price buys and what it leaves out.

You have a site, but it is slow, dated or rented

That is a website redesign. The difference from a new build is care for what already works: old web addresses get pointed at the new pages so you do not lose the search history you have built.

You have a site, but nobody finds it

Read SEO web design. It explains how titles, headings, schema, speed and town pages are built into the site from the start, instead of sold back to you as an add-on.

Web design services by trade

A trade page is not a different product. It is the same hand-written website, planned around how customers in that line of work search and decide. These are the trades with real search demand behind them, so each has its own page.

Contractors, plumbers and roofers

For the trades, the site exists to make the phone ring. The number sits at the top of every page, the services are named in the words customers use ("water heater replacement", not "hydronic solutions"), and the towns you drive to each get mentioned properly. See contractor website design, web design for plumbers and roofing contractor website design.

Law firms and therapists

Professional practices have rules a contractor does not. A law firm site has to explain practice areas without making promises the state bar would object to. A therapist's contact form should never ask a stranger for health details. See web design for law firms and web design for therapists.

What is the same in every service

Whatever the starting point, these parts of the work are the same for every business:

  • A demo first. You give your business name and trade on the demo page and see a site built for you before you pay anything.
  • Pages written by hand. No page is filled into a template from a list of town names. Maurice writes each one for your business.
  • Built for phones first. Google has crawled sites primarily with its mobile crawler since it finished the move to mobile-first indexing in 2023, and most of your customers are on a phone too.
  • Search basics included. Page titles, descriptions and a sitemap submitted to Google on every package. Google's own guidance treats a sitemap as a hint that helps it find your pages, not a ranking switch, and we describe it that way.
  • You own it. The finished site is yours outright once it is paid for.
  • A person you can reach. The phone is answered any hour by an AI receptionist that can put you through to Maurice, the same person who builds the site.

Which package goes with which service?

The services describe the kind of site. The packages describe how big it is. Any service can be built on any package, and here is the usual fit:

  • Starter, $497A new business, or a one-town business that needs five solid pages: home, services, about, contact and service area.
  • Pro, $997A business that takes calls from several towns and wants 10 pages, 10 researched city pages, schema and Google Business Profile setup.
  • Agency, $1,997A business with many services across a wide area, where keyword research decides which town-and-service pages are worth writing, usually 30 to 60.

All three are one-time prices. The full comparison, including what is monthly and what is not, is on the pricing page.
