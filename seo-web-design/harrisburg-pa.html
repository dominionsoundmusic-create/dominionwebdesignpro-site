How the build works

Most web design goes: deposit, contract, three weeks of silence, then a reveal you are too invested to argue with. We do the reverse. The site gets built up front and shown to you finished. The conversation about payment happens after you have seen whether it is any good.
Almost everyone who finds you in Harrisburg will do it on a phone, often outdoors, often on a signal that is not great. Every site we build is designed for that first and checked on a desktop afterwards — the opposite of how most sites are made, and the reason so many local business sites are slow and cramped.

Who we build for in Harrisburg

Most of the work we do in Harrisburg is for professional services, trades, logistics suppliers and restaurants feeding the government workforce. Different trades, same problem: the phone rings while you are busy, the enquiry arrives at nine at night, and the customer who could not reach you calls the next name on the list. A website will not fix a staffing problem, but it will stop the ones who never called from disappearing silently.
A lot of business here is done with the state or with people who work for it, and that audience checks whether you look legitimate before they call. Credibility does more work than flash in a capital city. A clean, current site reads as a real company; a dated one raises questions.
