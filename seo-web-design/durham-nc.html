Web design in Durham, and why local context matters

Durham runs on Duke University, Research Triangle Park and a startup and biotech economy layered over the old tobacco warehouses. That mix decides who your customers are and how they look for you, and it is why a website built for Durham should not read like one built for anywhere else. The independent businesses here cluster around American Tobacco Campus, Ninth Street and downtown around Main — and the ones doing well are usually the ones easiest to find and quickest to answer.

How the build works

Most web design goes: deposit, contract, three weeks of silence, then a reveal you are too invested to argue with. We do the reverse. The site gets built up front and shown to you finished. The conversation about payment happens after you have seen whether it is any good.
The phone is the real test. A Durham customer standing on a pavement with one bar of signal will not wait eight seconds for a homepage to appear, and will not pinch and zoom to find your number. Sites that fail that test lose the customer before you ever know they existed.

Who we build for in Durham

Most of the work we do in Durham is for restaurants, professional services, trades serving both historic homes and new construction, and B2B suppliers to the Triangle. Different trades, same problem: the phone rings while you are busy, the enquiry arrives at nine at night, and the customer who could not reach you calls the next name on the list. A website will not fix a staffing problem, but it will stop the ones who never called from disappearing silently.
Your customers here work for companies with serious design budgets. They notice a dated website the way you would notice a truck with a cracked windshield. The bar for what looks credible is higher in Durham than the price of a website suggests it should be.

Getting found in Durham

There is no trick to appearing in Durham searches. Google wants to know where you are, what you do, which areas you cover and whether anyone vouches for you. Most local business sites answer one of those four. We answer all of them as part of building the thing, because leaving it out and charging for it later is a poor way to treat a customer.

About a week, sometimes ten days. The slow part is almost always waiting on photos, so having those ready shortens it.

No. We build it first and show it to you finished. You pay when you are happy with it. That is the whole model.

Will it help me show up in Durham searches?

It is built to. We name the areas you actually serve and keep your details consistent, which is most of what local ranking rests on.
