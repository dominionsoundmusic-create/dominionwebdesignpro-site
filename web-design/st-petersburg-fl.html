Who we build for in St. Petersburg

Most of the work we do in St. Petersburg is for galleries, restaurants, contractors, med spas and the marine trades along the waterfront. Different trades, same problem: the phone rings while you are busy, the enquiry arrives at nine at night, and the customer who could not reach you calls the next name on the list. A website will not fix a staffing problem, but it will stop the ones who never called from disappearing silently.
Seasonal swing is real here. Winter brings snowbirds with money and time; August is quiet. A site has to sell in both halves of the year. Businesses here compete with Tampa across the bay, so being findable for St. Pete specifically is worth more than ranking vaguely for the metro.

Web design in St. Petersburg, and why local context matters

St. Petersburg runs on tourism, healthcare, marine science and a downtown arts scene that pulled a lot of small business into the city core. That mix decides who your customers are and how they look for you, and it is why a website built for St. Petersburg should not read like one built for anywhere else. The independent businesses here cluster around Central Avenue, the Grand Central District and the Edge District — and the ones doing well are usually the ones easiest to find and quickest to answer.

How the build works

The order of work is deliberately backwards from how most agencies do it. We build the site first — your photos, your words, your real hours. You look at a finished site for your business, not a mockup with your logo dropped into a template. If it is wrong, we change it. You pay when you are happy, and not before.
A site that looks impressive on a big monitor and falls apart on a phone is worse than no site at all, because it costs you the customer while looking like it is working. We build for the small screen first and let the desktop version follow.
