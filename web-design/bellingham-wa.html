Web design in Bellingham, and why local context matters

Bellingham runs on a port town between Seattle and Vancouver, running on marine trades, outdoor recreation and Western Washington University. That mix decides who your customers are and how they look for you, and it is why a website built for Bellingham should not read like one built for anywhere else. The independent businesses here cluster around Fairhaven's brick historic district and the downtown blocks around Railroad Avenue — and the ones doing well are usually the ones easiest to find and quickest to answer.

Who we build for in Bellingham

Most of the work we do in Bellingham is for outfitters, breweries, boat services, clinics and the trades that keep older housing stock standing. Different trades, same problem: the phone rings while you are busy, the enquiry arrives at nine at night, and the customer who could not reach you calls the next name on the list. A website will not fix a staffing problem, but it will stop the ones who never called from disappearing silently.
Half your customers are weekend visitors coming down from Vancouver or up from Seattle, and they decide where to eat and who to hire on a phone in the car. A site that loads fast on a mobile signal matters more here than in most towns, because so much of the searching happens on the road.

Getting found in Bellingham

Search visibility in Bellingham comes down to matching what a searcher nearby is actually asking for. That means naming your service areas explicitly, keeping your contact details identical across every listing, and giving Google enough real content to understand what you do. It is set up during the build, not quoted as an extra afterwards.
